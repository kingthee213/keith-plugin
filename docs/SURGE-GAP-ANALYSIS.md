# Keith — Surge and OB-Xf gap analysis

Compared against Keith's live surface in `docs/PARAM-MANIFEST.md` (111 automatable parameters, IDs 0–110) and `docs/MANUAL.md`. Reference trees: Surge XT (`surge-synthesizer/surge`, cloned at `.reference/surge` for this pass) and OB-Xf (`surge-synthesizer/OB-Xf`, `.reference/OB-Xf`). Rows are ranked in the order the owner named: analog voice, effects, modulation depth, wavetable oscillator. `build` = implement ourselves; `borrow` = GPL reuse is now approved and their code answers a concrete question; `skip` = out of scope for this instrument.

Keith already covers a usable subtractive core: two morph oscillators, sub, noise, FM, ring, sync, four filter types, three envelopes, unison, portamento, tape bus, two inserts, one master FX, and a four-slot matrix. The gaps below are what Surge or OB-Xf still have that Keith does not, or where Keith's version is plainly weaker.

## What Keith already does well enough

- **Tape bus** — wow, flutter, hiss, print, head bump (`fx_tape_*` in `PARAM-MANIFEST.md`). Surge has a tape effect (`fxt_tape`) but Keith's Mishby-oriented tape is a product feature, not a gap.
- **Key-tracked envelope times and stereo spread** — `synth_amp_attack_key`, `synth_stereo_spread`, etc. (U17). OB-Xf has slop and pan-per-voice but not this exact key-bend envelope editor.
- **Synthesized drum kit** — out of scope for Surge/OB-Xf comparison; not a gap.
